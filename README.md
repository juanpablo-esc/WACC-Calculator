This tool calculates a company's weighted average cost of capital, the rate used to discount free cash flows in a DCF. It applies CAPM to derive the cost of equity, adjusts the cost of debt for the tax shield, and weights each component by market value to produce a single blended rate. Beyond the point estimate, it runs WACC across a range of debt-to-total-capitalization ratios and identifies the ratio at which WACC is minimized, showing the trade-off between the cheaper, tax-advantaged cost of debt and the rising cost of equity and default risk that come with more leverage. Inputs and outputs are laid out step by step, making it easy to test sensitivity and document the assumptions behind a valuation.
